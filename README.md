1. The AWS Shared Responsibility Model means that both AWS and the customer have a role to play in keeping cloud resources secure. AWS is responsible for protecting the infrastructure itself, such as its data centers, physical servers, networking, and the services it provides. On the other hand, the customer is responsible for securing what they put on AWS, including their data, applications, passwords, permissions, and service configurations. In simple terms, AWS protects the **cloud**, while the customer is responsible for protecting what they **put in the cloud**.


3. This model matters because it helps businesses clearly understand who is responsible for protecting their systems and data, reducing security risks, mistakes, and costly breaches.
